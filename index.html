OPTIONAL · DISPLAY

Ambient visualizer

A colorful ambient display covers the app after five idle minutes. The Settings button and track-bar visualizer button request fullscreen to hide the title bar when your browser allows it. The track-bar button works even if automatic mode is off. Idle activation stays inside the app window. Click, tap, or press any key to return. With Reduce Motion enabled, the background fades gently while the records keep spinning.

OPTIONAL · LYRICS

Lyrics

To enable lyrics, sign in to Genius API clients ↗ and create a client. Name it RCRD, leave the icon URL blank, and use http://127.0.0.1:3000/ for the website. Leave the redirect URI blank if allowed; if required, use that same local URL. RCRD does not use a Genius sign-in redirect.

Add the resulting Client Access Token as GENIUS_ACCESS_TOKEN in your local .env, then restart RCRD. Select Lyrics in the track bar while a song is playing. RCRD searches Genius by title and artist only when you ask and does not store lyric text. Lyrics display in a Genius-hosted view when available, or you can open Genius directly. Genius may receive your IP address when the hosted view loads. Keep the token private; never paste it into the app or share it.

OPTIONAL · SPOTIFY

Your listening history

Spotify cannot send your lifetime history directly to RCRD. Request Extended Streaming History from Spotify ↗, then download its ZIP. Select it here once; RCRD calculates play counts locally. The ZIP is discarded after import.

Imported ZIP counts include music played for at least 30 seconds through the export date. Saved music adds recent Spotify play events from any device; Spotify may not return older plays missed between syncs. These are RCRD-recorded counts, not Spotify's official totals. Sign in to the matching Spotify account before importing. Some exports do not identify the account, so RCRD will ask you to confirm it. Counts stay on this computer until you delete them here; signing out or clearing browser data does not remove them.

    01 · FIRST RUN

    Get started

    1. Install Node.js from its official download page ↗ (choose the LTS release, version 18 or newer) and Google Chrome from Google ↗ on this computer. RCRD runs locally; it is not a hosted multi-user service.
    2. Create a Spotify developer app in the Spotify Developer Dashboard ↗. Spotify's app setup guide ↗ shows where to create the app and find its Client ID and Client Secret. In the app settings, add http://127.0.0.1:3000/auth/spotify/callback as a Redirect URI for RCRD's default port.
    3. Sign in to Discogs and open Discogs Developer settings ↗ (also under your profile → Settings → Developers). Register an application, set its callback URL to http://127.0.0.1:3000/auth/discogs/callback for the default port, and note its Consumer Key and Consumer Secret. Discogs' settings guide ↗ shows where the Developers area lives. RCRD needs the application credentials, not a personal API token.
    4. Copy .env.example to .env in the project folder. Put the Spotify values in SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID and SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET, and the Discogs values in DISCOGS_CONSUMER_KEY and DISCOGS_CONSUMER_SECRET. Keep the callback values consistent with the URLs registered above. Never share or paste your .env file into RCRD or a public repository.
    5. Double-click Launch RCRD.cmd to open RCRD, or use a taskbar shortcut if you have pinned one yourself. Select Connect Spotify and Connect Discogs to authorize your accounts. For normal use, close and reopen the app as needed; use Settings → Restart RCRD when the local server needs a restart.

    Spotify Premium is required for playback in the RCRD player. You can still use available browsing features without it.

    02 · SPOTIFY

    Listen & discover

    Listening shows recent music and lets you search Spotify. Saved music browses saved albums, tracks, and playlists; searching Saved library shows matching songs alongside albums. Only playlists you own or help manage appear here; other followed playlists are unavailable to this app. Change Search scope to search the entire Spotify catalog. Use Grid/List, sorting, and pagination to narrow a large library.

    The track bar controls playback, shuffle, repeat, progress, volume, and Liked Songs. Press Space to play or pause, Ctrl+Right for the next track, Ctrl+Left for the previous track, or Ctrl+Up/Down to adjust volume when you are not typing or using another control. Click the speaker icon to mute or restore the previous volume. Its playback-health control shows account connection, player readiness, and the active device. Choose a device there or use Retry player if RCRD's player is not ready.

    03 · DISCOGS

    Find the right edition

    Search an artist or album from the Listening panel, use Search in Discogs from a track, or open Explore catalog. Narrow results by artist, release title, catalog number, or barcode. Explore editions shows versions of a Discogs master release. Select two or three editions and choose Compare editions to check their differences on demand.

    Add a confirmed release to My collection or Wantlist. Choose 12, 24, or 48 records per page independently in My collection and Wantlist. A possible shelf match means RCRD found a similar album, not necessarily the same pressing—verify it before adding or skipping it. Wantlist’s Find editions searches the catalog; Shop on Discogs opens the selected release on Discogs, where sale listings may be available.

    OPTIONAL · PERSONALIZE RCRD

    Optional features

    Long-term favorites shows albums beyond recent plays on request. Spotify asks for an additional permission the first time; you can keep using Listening without it.

    RCRD Mix previews a 100-song mix related to the playing song. Nothing starts until you choose to replace playback. If fewer than 100 songs qualify, RCRD explains the shortfall instead of filling the mix with unrelated tracks. No additional listener account is needed.

    Recorded play counts start with your Extended Streaming History ZIP from Spotify. Import it in Settings → Your listening history; RCRD keeps a local count summary, not the raw ZIP. Saved music adds recent Spotify plays from any device while RCRD is active, but gaps in Spotify's recent-play feed can leave counts incomplete. Reimport a newer export to refresh the historical snapshot.

    Lyrics can display a Genius-hosted view for the playing song, with a direct Genius link as fallback. Setup is explained in Settings → Optional features → Lyrics. Genius may receive your IP address when the view opens; RCRD does not store lyric text and is not affiliated with Genius.

    Ambient visualizer can start from the track bar at any time. In Settings → Optional features → Ambient visualizer, you can also enable it after five minutes of inactivity. It is an animated display, not an audio spectrum analyzer.

    04 · SETTINGS

    Manage RCRD

    Select your profile image to see Spotify and Discogs connection status or connect an account, plus Settings, the Help & setup guide, Restart RCRD, and About RCRD. Select a connected account to open Settings, where you can sign out or switch accounts. Settings also lets you enable the idle visualizer or clear this browser's RCRD data. Restart asks for confirmation and briefly interrupts playback. Sign out of RCRD before connecting a different account. You may also need to sign out of the provider website in the same browser profile; RCRD sign-out does not clear those website cookies.

    Connections and preferences can persist on this computer. Keep its browser profile and the project's .data folder private. Clearing local RCRD data is separate from changing the developer credentials in .env.

    05 · HELP

    When something does not work

    • App will not open: confirm Node.js and Chrome are installed, then use RCRD.cmd to check status or restart. Do not stop an unrelated process using the same port.
    • Account will not connect: check that the registered provider callback URL exactly matches the value in .env, including host and port. Start authorization again from RCRD if it expired.
    • Spotify connects but will not play: check Spotify Premium and Playback health. If it says Playback permission needed, reconnect Spotify from Settings to approve playback; otherwise choose Retry player if offered. Also check that Web Playback SDK is enabled for your Spotify developer app.
    • Results look old: check the source and last-updated labels. Cached results remain browsable if a provider is temporarily unavailable; reopen the relevant view when service returns.

    For developer configuration, maintenance, and validation details, see ADMIN_GUIDE.md in the local project folder. That document is intentionally not served inside RCRD.
